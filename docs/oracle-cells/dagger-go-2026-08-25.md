## The root module

The whole program does not fit: RTA over the root's dependency closure
(buildkit, containerd, …) with `InstantiateGenerics` — which RTA
requires — was **OOM-killed at 20.5 GB with tests and at 20.7 GB
without**, on a 30 GB box (H-9). Graded instead by package subtree
(`--packages`), each a smaller, stated question:

| `root/core` | `./core/...` without tests | **nothing to root at**: `core` has no binaries; with tests it is the integration program above |
| `root/dagql` | `./dagql/...` with tests | **OOM-killed at 24.7 GB after 222 s** |

**Verdict for the root module: not gradeable on this box, under any
subtree.** Every subtree that has roots imports the engine, and the
engine's closure with `InstantiateGenerics` is ~21–25 GB of SSA. The
recorded cost of finding this out: five failed attempts, ~45 minutes.
