## Contradicted (44, all semantic, lane `scip`): one C-182 cfg twin — Hobbes', registered

`leaf/src/common/crypto.rs` writes `pub mod aead` twice: under `#[cfg(feature = "openssl-aead")]` (line 43)
and under aws-lc/ring (line 207). Every arm mints one id (C-182), and the node sits at the **first** arm,
which this build does not compile. rust-analyzer indexed the compiled arm, and since ADR-165 a call lane B
resolves onto any arm draws to the node. So each edge names the right item, but its line points into the
uncompiled arm: `AeadCipher.key_len` 101 (compiled 273) ×13, `nonce_len` 105 (277) ×8, `new` 65 (235) ×5,
`AeadEncryptor.encrypt` 136 (300) ×5, `AeadDecryptor.decrypt` 186 (335) ×5, `encryptor` 81 (251) ×4,
`decryptor` 90 (261) ×4. **Not the oracle's grain: these are Hobbes-wrong at line grain.** The limit is
registered and surfaced: the ingest's `rust-cfg-twins` record names 19 twins with `AeadCipher`'s defs
(60, 230). memchr and dagger had no twin whose later arm is a lane B answer, so this is the first graded cell
where C-182 costs precision. The route is Max's (`currently-open.md`).
