  the unnested control shows it absent. The landed discriminator is what
  the flags can support — begin spelled somewhere other than where it
  expands, and the two ends spelled on different lines or in different
  files. The doer probed both forms before choosing. The ADR is
  corrected to match the tree, not the reverse.
