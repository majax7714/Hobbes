> ## What I changed
> 
> **`diff.py` — the rule.** `reference_for(case, scalar)` is the one function: a case is graded against the cell's own gold when its inputs hold a non-finite value **or** when the scalar reference's own answer for it is not finite; every other case is graded against the scalar. The flag is `Special.non_finite` on the case table's data (the four specials carry it; `large` does not — its overflow is caught on the scalar's answer), with `SPECIAL_OF_CASE` turning a driver record's case name back into its pattern. The docstring carries the reason and the evidence read in the image — `COSINE:BF16` answering 1 where the scalar says NaN, `DOT:BF16` on `large` NaN on AVX2/AVX-512 and +inf on SSE2, AVX-512's f16 `l1`/`l2` agreeing where SSE2's and AVX2's do not. `compare_records` (was `_compare_records`, now public so the comparison is testable without a driver) takes the gold mapping, counts `scalar_cases`/`gold_cases` per slot, and puts `reference` — and the scalar's value alongside — into `first_failure`. A gold-referenced case the gold has no answer for is **failed, never passed**.
> 
> **`grade.py` — the gold outputs.** `grade_with_references` builds the gold once and runs its driver over every slot each ISA the run touches installs (`installed_slots`), **before the first body is filled in**, while the staged copy is still the target's own bytes; the records and the gold-against-scalar disagreements are cached per ISA in `GoldReference`. `grade()` is unchanged for its callers. A gold that does not build or dies in the differential is `GoldUnavailable`, its own type. Each `slots` entry gains `graded: {scalar, gold}`.
> 
> **`selftest.py` — reported, not hidden.** The report gains `disagreements` (per ISA and slot, both values, and the reference that case is graded against); `ok` does not depend on them. The table names the count, one line per `(isa, slot)`, and counts separately any row still graded against the *scalar* — that reading is the tolerance table's to answer for, which is how uint8's rows moved.
